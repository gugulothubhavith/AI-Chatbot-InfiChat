This policy governs what you may and may not do with InfiChat ("the Service"). It
forms part of the Terms of Service. Where this policy and the Terms conflict, the
Terms govern.

This policy exists for two reasons. The first is legal: some uses are unlawful,
and we will not host them. The second is practical: the Service routes requests
through third-party AI providers whose own terms bind us, and a small number of
abusive users can degrade the Service for everyone else.

## 1. Prohibited uses

### 1.1 Unlawful activity

You may not use the Service to plan, carry out, or facilitate anything unlawful in
any jurisdiction that applies to you or to us. This includes fraud, money
laundering, sanctions evasion, trafficking in persons or controlled substances,
and the sale of stolen data or credentials.

### 1.2 Harm to people

You may not use the Service to:

- Generate or distribute child sexual abuse material. **This is an absolute
  prohibition with no exception, and we report it to the relevant authorities.**
- Threaten, stalk, harass, or incite violence against any person or group.
- Produce sexual content depicting real, identifiable people without consent, or
  content sexualising minors in any form.
- Generate content designed to promote self-harm or suicide.
- Discriminate against or disparage people on the basis of a protected
  characteristic, including race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex,
  gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or age.

### 1.3 Weapons and dangerous materials

You may not use the Service to seek or produce operational instructions for
manufacturing weapons, explosives, or chemical, biological, radiological, or
nuclear materials. General educational, historical, and policy discussion is
permitted; actionable synthesis or construction detail is not.

### 1.4 Attacks on computer systems

You may not use the Service to develop or deploy malware, ransomware, botnets, or
denial-of-service tooling; to compromise systems you do not own or lack written
authorisation to test; to conduct credential stuffing or mass credential
harvesting; or to build tooling whose purpose is evading security controls.

**Security research is permitted** where you own the target system, hold explicit
written authorisation to test it, or are participating in a sanctioned exercise
such as a capture-the-flag competition or a bug bounty programme within its
stated scope. If you cannot point to that authorisation, treat the activity as
prohibited.

### 1.5 Deception and impersonation

You may not use the Service to:

- Impersonate any person or organisation, or misrepresent your affiliation.
- Generate phishing content, fraudulent websites, or spoofed communications.
- Produce disinformation, or synthetic media of real people intended to deceive.
- Represent AI-generated output as human-authored where doing so is deceptive or
  is prohibited by law, professional rules, or academic policy that applies to
  you.
- Manipulate reviews, polls, engagement metrics, or electoral processes.

### 1.6 Surveillance and profiling

You may not use the Service for mass surveillance, for tracking individuals
without their consent, for facial or biometric identification of private
individuals, for predictive policing, or for profiling people on the basis of
protected characteristics.

### 1.7 Consequential decisions without human review

You may not use the Service as the sole basis for decisions that materially affect
a person's rights, safety, health, or access to essential services. This
specifically includes medical diagnosis or treatment, legal advice, credit,
insurance, housing, employment or academic decisions, benefits eligibility, and
criminal justice determinations.

The Service produces text. It does not exercise judgement, and it does not know
when it is wrong. A qualified human must make and remain accountable for
decisions of this kind. See section 3.

### 1.8 Abuse of the Service itself

You may not:

- Circumvent rate limits, usage quotas, or plan restrictions, including by
  registering multiple accounts.
- Share credentials, resell access, or provide access to third parties outside
  the terms of your plan.
- Scrape the Service, or use automated means to extract data other than through
  a documented API within your plan's limits.
- Reverse engineer the Service to build a competing product, except where that
  right cannot lawfully be excluded.
- Use the Service's output to train a competing model.
- Upload malware, or content you lack the right to upload.
- Attempt to extract system prompts, other users' data, or credentials, or
  manipulate the Service into ignoring its own operating instructions.
- Interfere with the Service's availability or with other users' use of it.

### 1.9 Third-party provider terms

The Service routes requests to third-party AI providers listed in our
[Sub-processors](/legal/sub-processors) page. Their acceptable-use terms apply to
your use of the Service in addition to this policy. Conduct that breaches their
terms breaches this policy.

## 3. Output accuracy and your responsibility

This section is the one most likely to matter to you in practice.

**Model output is frequently wrong, and it is wrong in ways that read as
confident.** Large language models generate plausible text; they do not verify
facts. The Service may state falsehoods as fact, invent citations, misattribute
quotations, produce code with security flaws or subtle logic errors, and reason
incorrectly while presenting the reasoning fluently. Features named "deep
research" or "deep thinking" perform more steps than a single response; **they do
not make the output reliable**, and the citations they produce require checking
against the underlying sources.

Accordingly:

- **Verify anything you rely on.** Independently confirm factual claims,
  citations, quotations, figures, and legal or technical assertions before acting
  on them.
- **Review generated code before running it,** particularly where it touches
  authentication, cryptography, payments, personal data, deletion, or anything
  network-facing. Generated code may contain vulnerabilities.
- **Do not treat output as professional advice.** The Service does not provide
  medical, legal, financial, tax, or other professional advice, and no
  practitioner-client relationship arises from using it.
- **Keep a human in the loop** for any decision affecting a person.

Nothing in the Service's fluency should be read as a warranty of correctness. See
the disclaimer and liability sections of the Terms of Service.

## 5. Consequences of breach

Depending on severity, we may:

1. Issue a warning.
2. Remove or restrict access to specific content.
3. Restrict features or reduce limits.
4. Suspend the account.
5. Terminate the account.
6. Report the matter to law enforcement.

**We act proportionately, and we prefer a warning to a ban** where the breach
looks inadvertent and no one has been harmed. We reserve the right to act
immediately and without warning where there is a risk of harm to a person, a
credible legal threat to us, or a breach of section 1.2.

Termination for breach does not entitle you to a refund of fees already paid,
except where a refund is required by law.
